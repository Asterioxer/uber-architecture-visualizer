Space becomes a 64-bit number.

Every H3 hexagon has six equidistant neighbours, so a radius search is just rings of cells: gridDisk(origin, k). Geohash has two neighbour distances and S2 has four edge plus four corner neighbours.

Wait two seconds, match better.

Greedy first-come matching hands Rider 1 the nearest driver, even if that leaves Rider 2 waiting ten minutes. DISCO buffers requests for 2 to 5 seconds and solves the whole batch with the Hungarian algorithm in O(V³).

Surge shouldn't have cliffs.

Flink counts supply and demand per H3 cell. Raw, crossing one street can drop a 2.4x multiplier to 1.0x. A weighted kernel over neighbouring rings, M_smooth(h) = Σ w·M_raw ÷ Σ w, removes the cliff.

GPS lies between tall buildings.
